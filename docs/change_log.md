## [v1.0] — 2025-01-01

**Initial release.**

- Created repository structure: `docs/`, `templates/`, `scripts/`.
- Published `README.md` with overview, model summary, and maintenance ownership.
- Published `docs/merchant_onboarding_guide.md` — full internal operations guide covering business rules, batch model, registry model, status management, and SLA expectations.
- Published `docs/merchant_onboarding_checklist.md` — pre-batch operational checklist for Tuesday/Friday submissions.
- Published `docs/process_flow.md` — step-by-step process description with Mermaid flowchart and ownership matrix.
- Published `docs/email_templates.md` — standard templates for batch submission, follow-up, clarification, and merchant updates.
- Published `docs/decision_log.md` — initial decision log with seven founding decisions documented.
- Published `templates/merchant_registry_template.csv` — internal master registry template.
- Published `templates/sber_batch_template.csv` — Sberbank batch submission template.
- Published `scripts/generate_templates.py` — Python script to regenerate CSV templates.

**Author:** Process Owner / Registry Owner  
**Triggered by:** Sberbank request to systematise merchant submission process.
